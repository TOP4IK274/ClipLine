END-TERM PART C · DATA ANALYSIS IN THE PRODUCT (C1–C5)

Database Analytics (124 Verified Records)

Part C2 — Repeatable SQL Queries for Supabase SQL Editor:
-- Q1 (Bar Chart): Total Bookings & Revenue (KZT) by Service Category
SELECT s.name AS service_name, COUNT(a.appointment_id) AS total_bookings, SUM(s.price_kzt) AS revenue_kzt
FROM appointments a
JOIN appointment_services aps ON a.appointment_id = aps.appointment_id
JOIN services s ON aps.service_id = s.service_id
WHERE a.status != 'cancelled'
GROUP BY s.name ORDER BY total_bookings DESC;

-- Q2 (Line Chart): Peak Barbershop Demand by Hour of Day (Astana UTC+5)
SELECT EXTRACT(HOUR FROM (starts_at AT TIME ZONE 'Asia/Almaty')) AS hour_of_day, COUNT(*) AS bookings_count
FROM appointments
WHERE status != 'cancelled'
GROUP BY hour_of_day ORDER BY hour_of_day ASC;

-- Q3 (Table): Barber Completion Rate, Average Service Duration & Rating
SELECT b.barber_id, COUNT(a.appointment_id) AS total_assigned,
       SUM(CASE WHEN a.status = 'completed' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS completed_cuts,
       ROUND(AVG(s.duration_min), 1) AS avg_duration_min
FROM appointments a
JOIN appointment_services aps ON a.appointment_id = aps.appointment_id
JOIN services s ON aps.service_id = s.service_id
GROUP BY b.barber_id ORDER BY completed_cuts DESC;

LEGAL & DATA PRIVACY

Terms of Service & Personal Data Agreement

1. Compliance with Kazakhstan Legislation: In accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V "On Personal Data and Their Protection", ClipLine KZ collects only your name, verified email address, and mobile number strictly for scheduling barbershop appointments.

2. Cryptographic Storage & Access Control: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; all credentials use PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashing. Role-based access control (RBAC) isolates branch records so barbers only view their own assigned queue.

3. Anti-Abuse & Authentic Data Policy: Automated scraping, disposable email domains, and unverified phone numbers are blocked. Users agree to provide accurate contact details and cancel appointments at least 1 hour prior to the scheduled slot.

4. Partner Barbershop B2B Verification: Submitting a barbershop branch requires ownership authorization, a valid 12-digit KZ Business Identification Number (BIN/IIN), and explicit approval by ClipLine Head Administration.
